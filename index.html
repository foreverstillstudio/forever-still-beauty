Harder Than the Army

This album wasn't created in a perfect studio.

It was built in the middle of real life — in between raising my boys, cleaning up chaos, and carrying everything on my own.

For a long time, I thought I was missing someone. But the truth is... I was missing myself.

These songs are pieces of that journey — not learning how to stand, but remembering who I was before I had to be so strong all the time.

This isn't just music. It's what it sounds like to come back to life.

11 My Two Reasons ↓

Why I kept going when I had nothing left. This one is for my boys — the whole reason the rest of the album exists.

The person behind the music

Chrissy Schroer

Writer · Veteran · Mother — Forever Still is her voice

I didn't stumble into this — I built it.

I'm a U.S. Army veteran who stepped away from the world for nearly a decade to rebuild my life and protect my children after surviving serious trauma. I chose safety. I chose survival. I built a quiet, stable life from nothing.

This year, I made the decision to step back into the world — publicly, creatively, and without hiding.

AI didn't make me an artist — it gave me a way to keep creating while raising two young boys on my own. The story, the emotion, and the meaning have always been mine.
